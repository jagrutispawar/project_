About the System

A simple digital solution for everyday grocery shop operations.

Kirana Store Management System

This web application helps a small kirana shop owner maintain product records, track stock, identify items that need restocking and record sales in one place.

Objectives

  • Reduce manual record-keeping and calculation errors.
  • Keep product prices and quantities organised.
  • Identify low-stock products before they run out.
  • Maintain a basic sales history for review.

Technology Stack

PythonFlaskSQLiteHTMLCSS

Modules

  • Dashboard and summary statistics
  • Product and inventory management
  • Sales entry and automatic stock deduction
  • Low-stock alerts and sales history

Educational project: use sample data for demonstration. Authentication and production-grade security are not included in this version.
